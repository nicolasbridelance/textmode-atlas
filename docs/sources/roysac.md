# Source note: roysac.com

M0 note: access, limits and terms, checked at the source on 2026-10-09.

## What it is

The site of Carsten Cumbrowski (Roy/SAC), artist of the ANSI scene: galleries of ANSI and ASCII,
artpack downloads, editors and tools, scene history, and the largest known collection of
TheDraw fonts (`.TDF`, block-letter fonts drawn in ANSI), with a format specification and a
font tool. One person's site.

## What it holds for the museum

- **TheDraw fonts collection**: a single ZIP,
  `images/galleries/ZIP/ROYS-THEDRAW_TDF_FONTS_COLLECTION.ZIP`, **118.8 MB**, last modified
  2015-09-05; fonts grouped in `TDFONTS0.TDF`, `TDFONTS1.TDF`…, with preview images and a
  `CONTENT.TXT` index. Needed for lead I52 (reading block lettering by matching fonts cell by
  cell). The museum's corpus already holds 28 `.TDF` files (23 distinct) in 9 packs.
- **The `.TDF` specification**, on his blog: the reference for a reader.
- Downloads of packs and intros: Demozoo has 25 links to `roysac.com/images/galleries/DOWNLOAD/`.

## Access

Static HTTP. robots.txt allows all but `/error/` and `/roy_ansidetail.asp*`, and gives a contact:
carsten@cumbrowski.com.

## Terms

None stated on the fonts page. Each font has its own designer, often named inside the font file;
the collection is Roy/SAC's gathering of them.

## How the museum uses it

- **Ask first** (CLAUDE.md, prohibitions): a short message to Carsten Cumbrowski, sent by the
  owner, before downloading the collection: the fonts are used to *read* lettering in works
  (exact cell matching, the font's name as an `inferred` assertion with an `algo:` author), never
  to draw new lettering (invariant 9).
- Until then, I52 can start with the 23 fonts already in the corpus.
