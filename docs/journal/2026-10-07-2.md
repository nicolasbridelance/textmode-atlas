# 2026-10-07 — Session 2: the conservation renderer

## Goal

Clear the two Dependabot pull requests, then build the conservation renderer and compare it with
ansilove (spike 0001).

## Done

- **Dependabot.** #20 (actions group) merged. #11 (TypeScript 7) closed: the native compiler no
  longer ships the JS API that svelte-kit sync, svelte-check, typescript-eslint and knip call;
  Dependabot now ignores 7.x (#22).
- **Spike 0001** ([report](../spikes/0001-ansilove-parity.md)): on 122 files from five 16colo
  packs, kept local, rendering our grid matches ansilove on 96 of the 99 files in the VGA font.
  The rest: ansilove draws the SAUCE record as art when no EOF byte precedes it; two files use
  PabloDraw 24-bit colour, which our decoder skips. About 1 h of a 3 h box.
- **ADR 0010** and the foundation document amended: conservation PNGs are drawn from the grid by
  `tm_render`; ansilove stays pinned as the reference (#23).
- **Decoder fix**: the canvas ends at the last written row; SAUCE heights often count a trailing
  CR LF (#24).
- **Conservation renderer** `tm_render.conservation` (#26): font, settings from SAUCE or profile
  values, integer scale, 9th column, iCE / blink, recipe with `pixels_sha256` (the
  reproducibility key, equal to ansilove's on Horizon). `tests/golden/renderings.json` pins the
  golden pixels; `just parity` runs ansilove in the development image, in CI.
- `just hygiene` no longer reports `.git/` content as duplication (#25).

## Decisions

- Render from the grid, ansilove as an oracle (ADR 0010): one decoding for site, analysis and
  download. Recorded in the ADR, the spike report and the foundation document.
- The determinism key is the SHA-256 of the RGB pixels, not of the PNG file: it is independent
  of the encoder and comparable with ansilove. The PNG digest is recorded too; Pillow encodes it
  with its bundled zlib, pinned by `uv.lock`.
- The conservation PNG ignores the SAUCE aspect flag: aspect belongs to the authentic profile,
  and stretching would interpolate.
- Blinking cells are drawn lit, as ansilove does; the recipe says so (`blink_phase`).
