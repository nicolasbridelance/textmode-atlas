## Decisions

- Misclassified artifacts are corrected by the decoder's classified error, not by updating the
  immutable artifact row (invariant 1); a correction mechanism is known debt.
- Binary formats are recognised by their signatures, not by NUL bytes (1.5% of real `.ANS`
  hold NULs as blanks).
- Exploratory results on the train packs go into the works note and the leads, never as
  findings; confirmatory tests wait for pre-registration and the test packs.

## Working habits learned this session

- Kill background jobs by PID (`ps -eo pid,args | grep …`), never `pkill -f`: it matches the
  shell that runs it.
- The pre-push hook runs `just check` on the whole working tree: stash or lint untracked work
  before pushing another branch.
- Subjects of commit messages: 72 characters at most; the hook rejects the commit, and a
  pushed branch then lacks it.
- Look at screenshots of every visible change: the explorer's filter race and the noise grids
  were both found by looking.
