# 2026-10-08 — Session 4: all of 16colo, and the catalogue

## Goal

After the move to the larger codespace (8 cores, 31 GB, 63 GB disk): repair the environment,
ingest and decode all of 16colo, then roadmap step 2, the catalogue exploration.

## Done

- **Environment** after the rebuild: `just setup` installs Playwright's browser and system
  libraries (#40) and waits for the compose services when there is no Docker CLI (#41), so the
  `postCreateCommand` runs to the end. Session 3's journal merged (#39).
- **`expansion` table** (migration 0002, #42): the outcome of reading each archive (ok, partial
  with the unreadable names, classified error); before, it was only printed.
- **`tm dataset build`** (#43): datasets defined in `datasets/<name>/dataset.yaml` (query, key,
  typed and described columns), built in one read-only snapshot into Parquet plus a manifest;
  byte-identical builds, tested. First dataset: `catalogue` (packs and files of 16colo).
- **Exploration notebook** `research/exploration/catalogue.py` (#44), marimo + DuckDB + Altair.
- **Every art file gets a decoding result** (#45): ASCII through the ANSI decoder, other art
  formats `unsupported_format` under decoder `none`.
- **Duplicate archive entries** no longer stop ingestion (#46).
- **ZIP archives without a central directory** recovered by 7-Zip, checked by our CRC
  (ADR 0014, #49): 151 members of five packs.
- **All of 16colo ingested and decoded locally**: 5,858 archives → 5,766 packs, 217,670 files,
  115,156 art works; 111,405 grids, 3,682 `unsupported_format`, 68 empty, 1 too large; no art
  work without a result.
- **Catalogue described** (#48): [findings](../../research/exploration/catalogue.md) and
  proposed D1 strata; roadmap step 2 ticked.

## Decisions

- Pack reading outcomes get their own table rather than `decoding` rows: decoding is about
  grids. Added to the foundation document's schema additions (#42).
- Datasets declare their columns with type and description (the foundation document asks for
  "requête, colonnes"), and the build fails when the query disagrees or the key repeats.
- ASCII is decoded as ansilove draws it, at the SAUCE width or 80 columns with the same wrapping;
  measured with ansilove on 79-, 80- and 100-column lines. So no decoder change and no version
  bump. Wide ASCII without SAUCE wraps, as in ansilove.
- ZIP may go to 7-Zip when Python cannot open it, never unchecked (ADR 0014).
- Exploratory notebooks live in `research/exploration/`, apart from pre-registered hypotheses.
- **For the owner**: D1 strata proposed as four eras × five packs plus four rare cases by hand,
  about 24 packs instead of the 20 of the research programme. To confirm before step 4.

## Problems met

- The first full ingestion stopped after 5,200 packs on an archive listing one entry twice
  (fixed, #46); the rerun also filled `expansion` for earlier packs.
- `gh pr merge --squash` is refused by the repository: rebase merges only.
- Two catalogue builds during the ingestion differed; that showed the tables were read at
  different moments, hence the single snapshot.
- marimo exports leave a `__marimo__/` session cache that REUSE rejects: now ignored.
- I first planned a new ASCII decoder with its own width rule; measuring ansilove showed it would
  have broken parity with the reference.

## Field notes added

Packs released under several names (92 identical archives, five across two years); a pack
holding the same file twice; packs that draw logos with empty file names in their listing;
`READ THE INI FILE`, a tool default, as the most common SAUCE group; packs that survive only
as cut downloads (#50).
