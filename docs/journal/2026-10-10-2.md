# 2026-10-10 (2) — reading the words, a research room that grows

## Goal

The owner asked where the research programme stands on reading the text inside the works
("on a pas fait de NLP"), then to start testing, then to organise the research page so that
every study can be added.

## Done

- **Spike 0006** ([report](../spikes/0006-reading-the-words.md), PR #120): rules and offline
  tools (lingua, libphonenumber) over the text layer of `works` v6, train packs. Phone
  numbers (10,584 readings, 39 of 40 sampled right), URLs, credits and file-level language
  read well; greets names only about 52% precise. Spike code stays on the unmerged branch
  `spike/0006-reading-the-words` (local) until the step 15 ADR.
- **Research room as an index** (ADR 0029, PR #121): registry `research/studies.json`, an
  index filtered by programme strand, one page per study; the exploration moved to
  `/research/corpus`. The notes' Markdown now renders emphasis, code, web links and wrapped
  list items, and tables scroll on phones.

## Decisions

- ADR 0029: one registry for the research room, titles and summaries in en and fr, bodies
  in their original language. Empty strands are shown, so the room shows its gaps.
- Spike 0006 recommends, for the step 15 ADR: per-line readings signed `algo:`, phone
  numbers first, ambiguous places kept as candidates, zones before greets, and D2 labelled
  by a person.

## Problems

- Native pre-push fails on Windows (archive tools); pushed through the container, after
  recompiling Paraglide there. `git archive` does not delete files: a removed source must be
  removed in the container snapshot by hand.
- `vite preview` keeps its file list from start: after a rebuild, new assets are 404 until
  restarted (a false overflow on phones came from it).
