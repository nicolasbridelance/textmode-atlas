# 2026-10-10 (3) — a museum of every character art

## Goal

The owner asked to keep enriching the corpus, then widened the aim: "le musée des arts du
caractère", with OS and software screens, splash and loading screens, games, Minitel,
walkthroughs, NFO, readme, manuals, man pages, warez, 404 pages; the holdings far from complete,
representative, or even holding one work of each practice. He asked for alternative sources
(Wayback Machine, text files found anywhere, forums, searches), for the museum to take its own
"photographs" of textual art, and for every addition to show on the site and in the EDA.

## Done

- **Scope**: ADR 0030 (screens and documents of text-mode computing, never a cracked or
  commercial program), ADR 0031 (a registry of practices, one representative first), ADR 0032
  (excerpts: the museum cuts the textual artwork where it finds it, credited, withdrawable; the
  owner's decision). Foundation document amended each time.
- **Registry** `corpus/practices.yaml`: 92 practices in 15 families, with how each can be held
  and its representative; `tm corpus practices` and `practices.json` for the site.
- **Acquisitions**: `tm acquire` reads `corpus/acquisitions.yaml` (source, basis, bilingual
  reason, checked at the source) and fetches one file at a time; excerpts cut lines of a page
  (HTML read as a reader saw it, a pattern, C string escapes). 33 files: PETSCII, ATASCII, Amiga
  ANSI and Mode 7 compo winners, a textmode demo, a 1 KB intro, a 256-byte game, a 1990
  cracktro (checked to be the intro alone), DECUS printer art, ANSI music, a walkthrough, a
  courier text, `ls(1)`, IOCCC 1988, Befunge, the oldschool PC fonts, Fahlman's 1982 smiley,
  the FreeBSD daemon, NetHack's tombstone, a 2channel AA, the 3611 Minitel directory, and
  Commons reproductions (Apollinaire, Mallarmé, micrography, Jacquard, punched card, sampler,
  mosaic, Chaplin typed, Guru Meditation, Midnight Commander, a split-flap board).
  **51 of 92 practices are held; no family is empty.**
- **Rights and export**: `can_display()` shows scene releases, licensed or public-domain works
  and excerpts; `tm export` credits works in no pack; the cartel names the licence.
- **Site**: a practices room (`/practices`), an eighth EDA chapter (breadth: practices by family,
  works by source and by basis), snapshot schema 3 published.
- **textfiles.com**: the `ascii/`, `acid/` and `ice/` trees mirrored and ingested (1,052 packs,
  2,633 loose files), 15,222 acquisitions recorded; an archive reader crash fixed (negative
  offset, `jasper06.zip`).
- **Windows**: `just check` passes natively (arj test skipped where arj cannot exist, reuse's
  charset-normalizer extra); pushes no longer need the Linux container. Old PR #107 (works v6)
  merged.

## Decisions

- ADR 0030, 0031, 0032 as above; excerpts and licensed works are shown, credited, withdrawable.
- Release dates of acquisitions stay in the manifest's text until `date_basis` allows a source date.
- A manifest may correct the art kind of a file it acquired (`format` is not immutable).

## Problems

- Pushing through the container failed four times (pyright stricter on Linux, REUSE for derived
  JSON, knip/ESLint, Paraglide not recompiled); now native.
- Python heredocs turned `\n` escapes into line breaks in code; edited by hand.
- A bare `uv sync` removed the museum host's dependencies; `uv sync --all-groups` restored them.
- `just shots` cannot start its server on Windows; screenshots taken with a local preview.
- The Internet Archive was offline: no Wayback excerpt yet.
